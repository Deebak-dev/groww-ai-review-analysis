# Weekly Product Pulse — Groww

## 1. Review Period and Dataset

- **Review period:** October 6–10, 2026
- **Total review count:** 623

## 2. Key Customer Themes

### General Satisfaction — Positive
Users praise Groww for its intuitive, user-friendly interface, particularly for beginners and SIP investments.

### Infrastructure and System Stability — Negative
Users reported a critical “No healthy upstream” error on October 6 that prevented access to both the app and website. Other complaints included app hangs, launch failures following updates, and missing landscape support for Android tablets.

### Conversion and Onboarding Friction — Negative
Reported issues included non-clickable terms-and-conditions checkboxes during IPO applications and birth-certificate upload failures for minor accounts.

### Financial Transparency and Support Trust — Negative
Customers reported unexplained deductions, confusing MTF charges, and delays in support and verification. Some allegations require investigation and should not be treated as established facts.

### Professional Tooling Gaps — Mixed
Active traders requested Chart Replay for backtesting, automatic trailing stop losses, and a more granular market-cap breakdown in the mutual fund dashboard.

## 4. Fee and Brokerage Concerns

### Customer perceptions
Some reviewers consider brokerage and delivery charges expensive and report difficulty understanding deductions. These concerns indicate a need for clearer explanations; they do not, by themselves, establish incorrect billing.

### Official references — not independently verified for this report
- [Groww Pricing](https://groww.in/pricing)

Individual fee disputes should be checked against the relevant transaction records and contract notes.

## 5. Recommended Actions

1. **Audit infrastructure resilience:** Investigate the “No healthy upstream” incident and post-update stability issues. Identify root causes and reduce the risk of users being locked out.
2. **Fix high-intent funnel bugs:** Prioritize the IPO terms-and-conditions checkbox and minor-account document-upload failures.
3. **Improve fee transparency:** Explore an in-app, plain-language breakdown of transaction charges that explains each deduction and links to the relevant transaction record.

## 6. Data Limitations
